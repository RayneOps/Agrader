Soil-based crop planning for smallholder farmers in Niger State.

The problem

Farmers choose which crops to plant without knowing the condition of their soil. They also cannot see how past seasons have changed it. So each season's choice is made without the facts that matter most.

How it works

  1. We test the soil on the farm.
  2. We record the farm's history and the crops the farmer wants to grow.
  3. We rank those crops and explain why.

Seasonal Soil Memory

Each farm keeps a season-by-season record of its soil and what was planted. A returning farmer never starts from zero, so the advice improves on every visit.

Stage

Prototype. Not yet in use with farmers.
